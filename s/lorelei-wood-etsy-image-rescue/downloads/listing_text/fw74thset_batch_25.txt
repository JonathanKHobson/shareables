Title:
Medium plush "Waffles in the Garden" vintage chenille bathrobe upcycled from hand tufted chenille bedspreads

Description:
The vintage chenille bedspread used to create this bathrobe is a 'double' chenille, where
the background chenille (usually white) is matching generated, and the designs are
tufted on top. The background chenille in this bathrobe is a 'waffle' chenille, if you zoom
in you can see it is square. The hand tufting takes the form of the pretty flowers and
bows on top of the background chenille.
This bathrobe is very symmetrical, as that is how the bedspread was designed. I love to
create bathrobes out of this design, as it is easier to match the connecting parts. A
medium can fit up to a woman's size 12, with pockets; machine washable too!
Chenille bedspreads were created and manufactured in the US during the 1930s to the
1950s; much of it was hand tufted. The hand tufting process was often a series of knots
tied in a pre-printed design onto cotton (muslin) fabric. These one-of-a-kind bedspreads
varied by the tufter, as well as by materials used.
