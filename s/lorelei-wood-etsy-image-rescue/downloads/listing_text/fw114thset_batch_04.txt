Title:
Small heavy "Dusty Rose Quadruple Peacock" chenille bathrobe upcycled from vintage hand tufted chenille bedspreads

Description:
This small can fit up to a woman's size 8, with pockets. Four (!) peacocks...large one in
the back, another large one AND two more tiny ones in the front! The chenille used in
this robe is sturdy and heavier than most, keep you nice and toasty! Each of the FOUR
peacocks is in beautiful colors of peach, blue, red and green. The sleeves match the
peach in the peacocks, and the green leaves match too! Peacocks coming and going...
All vintage fabrics have a history prior to being upcycled, some were more loved than
others. The majority of my creations are made from vintage fabrics, and I wash/dry them
prior to upcycling. I check each linen carefully, and work around any stains, pinholes
and/or missing chenille. I am human, however; and sometimes miss things. I document
the issues and relay them in the description. I did not notice any problems in this
garment.
