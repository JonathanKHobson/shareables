Medium heavy "Two Giant Peacocks" vintage chenille bathrobe upcycled from hand tufted chenille bedspreads

Description:
Plushly tufted 'double chenille'; which means that there is 'background' chenille created
by a manufacturer, and the design (in this case the peacock and other colorful designs)
were hand tufted on top of the manufactured chenille. Not just one, but two gorgeous
giant peacocks. One peacock on the front, and another on the back of this robe.
Throughout the design, there is a beautiful sea glass green ribbon running through the
design, so I use that same color for the sleeves to pick up that color.
Medium can fit up to a woman's size 12, with pockets; machine washable too.
Chenille bedspreads were created and manufactured in the US during the 1930s to the
1950s; much of it was hand tufted. The hand tufting process was often a series of knots
tied in a pre-printed design onto cotton (muslin) fabric. These one-of-a-kind bedspreads
varied by the tufter, as well as by materials used.
