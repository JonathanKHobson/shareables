Small plushly tufted and heavy "Lollipop Flower Garden" vintage chenille bathrobe upcycled from hand tufted chenille bedspreads

Description:
Very heavy chenille, not a summer robe for sure! Thickly tufted and heavy, you tiny gals
will love this one...keep you toasty warm in vintage style.
Most vintage chenille flowers take the shape of the 'daisy', but not this one! A much
more rare 'lollipop' flower design, and tufted so thick and heavy its more like the chenille
in a letter sweaters (if you're old enough to remember those!). The original bedspread
had those beautiful scallops across the design, and I was able to capture the waves AND
match them closely at the side seams too! This bathrobe looks like it was originally
intended to be a bathrobe, not an upcycled bedspread...zoom in to those side seam pics
to see.
The sea of scallops that I was able to match across the design is matched by the pink in
the sleeves, a perfect match. Small can fit up to a woman's size 8, with pockets; machine
washable too.
