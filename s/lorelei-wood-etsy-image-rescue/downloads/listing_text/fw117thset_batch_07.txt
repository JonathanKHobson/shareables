Title:
Medium plush and soft "Daisies at Sunset" vintage chenille bathrobe upcycled from rare purple hand tufted chenille bedspreads

Description:
The chenille this bathrobe was created from is soft and plush; in excellent vintage shape.
Purple on purple, very rare; not many of this color combo in my shop or in existance,
especially in this great shape. Fran Drescher of 'The Nanny' would have loved this robe,
just her style. Lots of purple and white curly cues all over, and yellow daisies to boot!
The front of the robe is a bit 'high/low' since that end of the bedspread was curved, I
captured the curve at the front of the robe panels.
White sleeves with more curly cues, AND some green leaves and vines as well. Deep
pockets on both sides make it functional and warm. Medium can fit up to a woman's size
12, machine washable too.
Chenille bedspreads were created and manufactured in the US during the 1930s to the
1950s; much of it was hand tufted. The hand tufting process was often a series of knots
tied in a pre-printed design onto cotton (muslin) fabric. These one-of-a-kind bedspreads
varied by the tufter, as well as by materials used.
