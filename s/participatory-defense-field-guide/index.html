02 / A CALMER WORKFLOW

Make the next useful move.

One source connector, one guardrail skill, and one clear human handoff. Keep the workflow calm and auditable.

  1. 01

    Orient

    Identify the jurisdiction, stage, relevant date, representation, and immediate concern. Do not guess.

  2. 02

    Organize

    Separate facts, allegations, evidence, inferences, contradictions, and unknowns in a working copy.

  3. 03

    Verify

    Open the actual primary or official source. Record the pinpoint, date, and what remains uncertain.

  4. 04

    Hand off

    Bring a concise question list to a public defender, legal-aid provider, law library, clerk, or licensed attorney.
